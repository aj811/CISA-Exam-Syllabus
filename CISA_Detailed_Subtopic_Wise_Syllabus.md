# DOMAIN 1 --- INFORMATION SYSTEMS AUDITING PROCESS {#domain-1--information-systems-auditing-process}

## Weight: 18%

## A. PLANNING {#a-planning}

### 1A1. IS Audit Standards, Guidelines, and Codes of Ethics {#1a1-is-audit-standards-guidelines-and-codes-of-ethics}

**Study checklist**

-   IS audit standards, guidelines and ethics
-   Auditor independence and objectivity
-   Professional competence and due professional care
-   Confidentiality and conflicts of interest
-   Audit charter, authority and mandate
-   Auditor vs management responsibilities
-   Professional skepticism

**QAE focus:** independence, ethics, competence, due care, auditor
responsibility.

### 1A2. Types of Audits, Assessments, and Reviews {#1a2-types-of-audits-assessments-and-reviews}

**Study checklist**

-   Compliance, financial and operational audits
-   IT/general controls and application audits
-   Integrated, internal and external audits
-   Regulatory audits and forensic examinations
-   Assessments vs audits vs reviews
-   Engagement objectives, scope and assurance

**QAE focus:** identify the engagement type and appropriate audit
objective.

### 1A3. Risk-Based Audit Planning {#1a3-risk-based-audit-planning}

**Study checklist**

-   Audit universe
-   Risk-based audit approach
-   Business objectives and IT risks
-   Inherent and residual risk
-   Likelihood and impact
-   Qualitative and quantitative risk assessment
-   Risk ranking and prioritization
-   Scope, objectives, timing and resources
-   Audit program
-   Materiality and significance

**QAE focus:** FIRST steps, risk prioritization, scope and business
understanding.

### 1A4. Types of Controls and Considerations {#1a4-types-of-controls-and-considerations}

**Study checklist**

-   Preventive, detective, corrective, directive and deterrent controls
-   Compensating controls
-   Manual, automated and IT-dependent manual controls
-   General and application controls
-   Administrative, technical and physical controls
-   Control objectives
-   Control design vs operating effectiveness

**QAE focus:** identify the control type and match the control to the
risk.

# CISA QUESTION-READING CHECKLIST

Before selecting an answer, ask:

1.  What exactly is the question asking?
2.  Is it asking for FIRST, NEXT, BEST, MOST or LEAST?
3.  What is the underlying risk/problem?
4.  Which official domain/subtopic is being tested?
5.  Who is responsible?
6.  Is this governance or management?
7.  Is the answer preventive, detective or corrective?
8.  Is the question about a policy, process, control, evidence or audit
    activity?
9.  Which answer directly addresses the stated objective?
10. Are the other answers true but less relevant?

# FINAL STUDY RULE

Use the **official ISACA Exam Content Outline as the syllabus
boundary**.

Use detailed notes, examples and the official QAE to learn how each
official subtopic is tested.

Do **not** assume that every unfamiliar term appearing inside a QAE
scenario is a new official syllabus topic.

Examples:

-   **Cryptocurrency miner** can appear inside a security scenario; it
    is not a separate CISA subtopic.
-   **Short-term IT planning** can be tested under IT Resource
    Management; it is not a separate Domain 2 heading.
-   **Software escrow** can be tested under IT Vendor Management; it is
    not a separate Domain 2 heading.
-   **BIA/RTO/RPO/BCP/DRP** are explicitly part of Domain 4 Business
